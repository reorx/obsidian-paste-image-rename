# 1.7.2

A focused compatibility cleanup for setting labels. The sixteen plain-text labels
now use the older public name-element and chaining APIs, avoiding the Community
scanner's incorrect treatment of the overloaded setName method.

No labels, settings/defaults, controls, click callbacks, link resolution, editor
transactions, rename logic, or deletion safeguards change. Minimum Obsidian remains
0.12.0. The whole-vault experiment is not included.

## Verification and remaining advisories

- 179 runtime/regression tests and 20 release-policy tests pass.
- TypeScript, production build, and release-asset checks pass.
- Official Community ESLint 0.4.2: **0 errors, 35 warnings**, down from 51 warnings.
  API-version warnings decrease from **41 to 25**; Stylelint remains **0 errors
  and 0 warnings**. No lint rule overrides or suppressions were added.
- The 25 remaining API warnings concern host button callbacks, link resolution,
  and editor transactions. Their existing behavior is deliberately retained;
  replacing them just to silence a warning could affect click handling, filenames
  selected by links, or undo behavior.
- Historical SDK declarations do not certify corresponding old app runtimes.
  Existing capability guards and fallbacks remain in place.

See the [audit](https://github.com/reorx/obsidian-paste-image-rename/blob/1.7.2/docs/community-review.md)
for the verified overload issue and remaining warnings. This does not guarantee a
future Community scan or manual approval; another patch awaits new review feedback.
